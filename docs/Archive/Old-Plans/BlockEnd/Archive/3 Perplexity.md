- **Investors**: Transactional, not emotional. Less evangelic. Prefer to keep good deals private (no referrals).
- **Brokers**: Zero viral potential. B2B plays don't evangelize.

- **First-Time Buyers**: After 10K closes, EstateForge owns 10K actual transaction prices + buyer behavior patterns + satisfaction scores. This dataset is **irreplicable**. Competitors can never catch up.

→ **First-time buyers = strongest, most durable moat.**

**4. Market Size & Revenue Clarity**

- **First-Time Buyers**: 10M+/year. ₹90K per close. 500 closes/year = ₹4.5 Cr (simple math, proven).
- **Investors**: 1-2M/year (10x smaller). ₹25L per close, but 50 closes/year = ₹1.25 Cr (less total revenue initially).

RERA 2.0 (2025) mandates escrow protection, defect liability, transparency.[9] **First-time buyers are most affected** (scared of defects, want protection). EstateForge's trust-first positioning is perfectly aligned.

→ **First-time buyers benefit most from regulatory tailwind.**
