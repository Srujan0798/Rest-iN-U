1. Brutal review of today’s real-estate apps and why they fail users

Zillow, Redfin, 99acres, MagicBricks, [Housing.com](http://housing.com/) all pretend to be marketplaces.
They are not. They are lead-selling machines.

They fail in four lethal ways:

They show inventory, not truth.
Listings are duplicated, outdated, manipulated, or bait. Price history is hidden. Negotiated deals vanish. In India this is worse: brokers post fake photos, fake “ready to move”, fake approvals, fake urgency.

They push brokers instead of protecting buyers.
The moment you click, your phone is sold to agents. You lose control. Now you’re hunted. No neutral intelligence remains.

They give data without judgment.
Even when they show price, area, or maps, there is no answer to the only real question:

> “Is this a good decision for me?”
> 

No one tells you:

this building will leak

this developer is risky

this road will be blocked

this resale will be hard

this loan will trap you

They create anxiety, not confidence.
People don’t feel smarter after browsing. They feel more confused.
Too many options. No truth. No defense.

Real estate is a life decision.
All apps treat it like shopping for shoes.

That is the gap EstateForge attacks.
