**War Room Concept (From Deepseek):**
- Agents convene for each property
- Visible disagreements highlighted
- "Property Health Score" (0-100) with reasoning

```
Valuation Agent: "This listing is 18% ABOVE the last 6 comparable sales."
Legal Sentinel: "Builder has 2 ongoing delay cases in RERA tribunal."
Lifestyle Analyst: "Counter-point: Within 500m of planned metro - strong premium factor."
Conductor to User: "High price + some risk, but future connectivity is major plus.
                     SWARM VERDICT: Only worthwhile if Negotiation Strategist secures 15% discount. Proceed?"
```

**Property Health Score Example:**
> "Score 72: Strong on location (85) but concerns on builder reputation (65)."
